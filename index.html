How to Use ▼
1. Set your fare — Enter your transport cost per km in the Transport Fare card above.
2. Create a goal — Tap + New Goal on the Home tab. Give it a name and a target amount.
3. Log a walk — Each active goal card has a date and km field. Enter your distance and tap Log →. Savings are calculated automatically.
4. Track progress — The goal card shows your progress bar, earned amount, and days active.
5. Walk Log — The Walk Log tab shows all your walks with totals, tagged to the goal they were logged under.
6. Goal History — Completed goals move to the Goals tab. Achieved goals show ✓, abandoned goals show ✕.
7. Cross-device sync — Sign in from the Account card above. Your data syncs automatically across all your devices.
About Walk2Save

Every step you take instead of hopping on a bus or ride is money saved. Walk2Save turns that invisible saving into something you can actually see — set a goal, log your walks, and watch your progress build day by day. Simple, honest, and built for the habit of walking.
